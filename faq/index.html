What is PeptiCore?

PeptiCore is a research-use peptide catalog with product series, SKU-level vial specifications, visible starting prices, COA access where available, and direct WhatsApp inquiry.

Are PeptiCore products for research use only?

Research use only. Not for human consumption. Intended solely for laboratory research.

How do I request a COA?

Open a product series, review the available COA preview where listed, and include the requested SKU in your WhatsApp inquiry if you need documentation before a final quote.

How are specifications listed?

Specifications are listed by SKU, vial format, pack quantity, visible starting price, and COA availability where a batch document is linked.

Are listed prices final?

Listed prices are visible starting prices for catalog review. Final quote, shipping, and availability are confirmed by PeptiCore sales after your inquiry.

How does WhatsApp ordering work?

Build an order list in the catalog, review the estimated subtotal, then send the structured inquiry through WhatsApp for sales confirmation.

How do member points work?

Member points are shown inside the account center and are calculated by the active membership rules shown on the site.

Does fixed shipping count toward points?

Fixed shipping is shown separately from product subtotal. Points estimates are based on the active membership rules and the product amount shown in checkout.

Can I search by SKU or abbreviation?

Yes. The catalog search supports product names, abbreviations, SKU codes, and common specification formats.
