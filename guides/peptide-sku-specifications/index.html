Series, SKU, and specification

The series name identifies the product family. The SKU identifies one catalog record. The specification describes fields such as vial size, volume, or pack quantity that distinguish that record.

Why exact references matter

Several SKUs can share a series name. Using only that name can leave the required size unclear, while a SKU plus specification creates an unambiguous catalog reference.

Prices and documentation

PeptidesPrescripts shows starting prices and COA availability next to each SKU. Final quote, availability, and shipping are confirmed by sales after the selected records are submitted.

Building an inquiry list

Search by series, abbreviation, SKU, or specification; compare rows; add the intended record; and review the structured list before opening the WhatsApp inquiry.

Common interpretation errors

Do not use a series name as a substitute for an exact SKU. Similar names can contain different vial sizes, pack formats, prices, and documentation mappings.

Frequently asked questions

Is a product series the same as a SKU?

A series can contain several SKU records with different specifications.

What fields identify a PeptidesPrescripts catalog record?

Use the SKU code together with the displayed specification and series name.

Are starting prices final?

They are catalog references; PeptidesPrescripts sales confirms the final quote and availability.
