Chapter I · Company profile

PeptiCore is operated by Guangzhou AOSAI Biotechnology from one Guangzhou address: 177 Huancheng South Road. Product specifications, release checks, labeling, and dispatch are managed under one chain of custody so each order can be matched to a clear SKU and lot record.

Six operating standards.

Used for catalog review, documentation presentation, and customer communication.

01
Quality first
Specifications, release checks, and documentation come before speed.
02
Scientific rigor

03
Data accuracy

Chapter III · Inside the catalog

One catalog. Four review steps.

Catalog review, documentation access, SKU identification, and inquiry support are presented as one connected customer workflow.

01 / Catalog

Specification Mapping

Each catalog record keeps the product series, SKU code, listed format, and starting-price reference together for clear review.

02 / Documents

Documentation Review

Available COA previews and analytical-documentation context are connected to the catalog workflow where records are mapped.

03 / Records

SKU-Level Identification

Distinct SKU codes keep similar series and specifications separate during document review and order-list preparation.

04 / Inquiry

Sales Confirmation

Availability, shipping, and the final quote are confirmed by PeptiCore sales after the selected records are submitted.
